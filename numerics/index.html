Live PDE surrogates

Each page solves a parametric PDE as you move the controls. The solver behind it is the same in every case: one coarse-grid solve of the real equation, then a learned local polynomial correction on every patch of the fine grid, and optionally a few iterations of the real fine-grid solver started from that prediction. Milliseconds instead of seconds; no neural network.

Steady Navier–Stokes

A forced vortex flow in a box. Sliders for the forcing modes and the viscosity (Re 65 to 220); a 32×32 Newton solve plus local corrections gives the 128×128 flow. Compare with the full Newton solver.

Diffusion with a draggable object

Drag an inclusion through a heterogeneous medium and reshape its boundary with control points; the solution and the flux follow. Contrast 0.01 to 100. One model for every shape and position.

3D diffusion, 64³

A three-dimensional parametric coefficient; scrub through the volume with slice sliders. Here the surrogate is tens of milliseconds against about a second for the full solve.

Method: a two-grid local polynomial surrogate (coarse solve, fixed local residual bases from a few thousand training solutions, least-squares polynomial correctors per patch, fine multigrid-preconditioned CG from the prediction). Errors are measured against the fine-grid solver on held-out parameters; press "compare with the full solver" on any page to see them. Jakob Zech, Heidelberg, 2026.
