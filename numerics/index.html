Live PDE surrogates

Each page solves a parametric PDE as you move the controls, through a fast surrogate of the full solver. Press "compare with the full solver" on any page to see the reference solution, both times and the error.

Steady Navier–Stokes

A forced vortex flow in a box. Sliders for the forcing modes and the viscosity (Re 65 to 220); the 128×128 flow follows live.

Diffusion with a draggable object

Drag an inclusion through a heterogeneous medium and reshape its boundary with control points; the solution and the flux follow. Contrast 0.01 to 100. One model for every shape and position.

3D diffusion, 64³

A three-dimensional parametric coefficient; scrub through the volume with slice sliders. Tens of milliseconds against about a second for the full solve.
